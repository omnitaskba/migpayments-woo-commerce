WooCommerce crypto payment options.

== Description ==

The "Migpayments Crypto Gateway for WooCommerce" plugin enhances your WooCommerce-powered online store by seamlessly integrating cryptocurrency payment options into your checkout process.
With this plugin, customers can conveniently make purchases using a variety of cryptocurrencies, enhancing the flexibility of payment methods available to them.
